Why study at Ashesi

You learn how to think, work with others and act with integrity, whatever you choose to study.

Ethical leadership

Every student takes courses in leadership and ethics, and lives by an Honor Code that students help uphold.

Entrepreneurial mindset

Projects, internships and campus ventures give you practice at turning ideas into solutions for real communities.

Critical thinking

A broad core curriculum builds your ability to question, analyze and communicate clearly across disciplines.
